## [Unreleased]

## [0.1.2] - 2026-10-05

### Fixed

- **A resumed iOS app fills its screen again** — `display/visible-viewport`: an installed iOS app brought back from the background no longer comes up as a sliver of its shell over the background colour. A strip below the visible window is believed only while something is being typed into, and a resume or a rotation is measured again once the browser has settled.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-04

### Fixed

- **A canvas in the HUD comes into the picture** — `readHudLayer` now carries every canvas inside the HUD (a minimap) into the screenshot as the picture it holds, where it used to come out as an empty box.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-28

### Added

- The first cut of the framework, extracted from the three sibling racing games (game2, game3, game4): `core` (the seeded PRNG, the bit-exact math, value noise, heightfields, polylines, the sun and the moon, the clock, the output module), `racing` (the control tape ghosts and replays are kept on, the record book's policy, the standings), `audio` (the synthesized WebAudio instrument, its racks, banks and fader views), `pwa` (the service-worker update watch), `display` (the canvas's two sizes and the visible window), `shots` (the screenshot roll, its store, thumbnails, sharing, stamp and HUD layer), `input` (the thumb guard, the HUD press, the menu cursor), `hud` (the race clock and the figures a HUD writes, the count) and `loop` (the fixed-step run clock), plus the Node lab shelf under `tooling/` and the release and skill-lesson plumbing as `ogf-*` bins.
- `shots`: `copyWhenReady` falls back to writing the finished blob where the promise-form clipboard write is refused, and `copiedWithin` bounds how long a receipt waits on a clipboard that never answers.

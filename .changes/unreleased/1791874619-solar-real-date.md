title: The sun and the moon on a real date
---

`core/solar` reckons the sun's declination and the equation of time (NOAA's series after Meeus), the air's refraction, upper-limb sunrise and twilight windows that hold under the midnight sun and the polar night, and the moon off its real orbit (Schlyter's series, with the parallax) for any latitude, longitude and date — new functions beside the old ones, which are unchanged.
